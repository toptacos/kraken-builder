# Kraken brand guidelines (use this, not a new mark)

## Name
**Kraken** is a TopTaco product on topta.co. TopTaco is a brand of McAchran Consulting LLC. The tentacle-K lockup is the only app icon.

**Tagline:** One binary. Many tentacles.  
**URL:** https://kraken.topta.co  
**API:** https://api.topta.co

## Voice
Short. Technical. Second person. No lore paragraphs.  
Good: “Install once. Add a tentacle. Same JSON on every machine.”  
Bad: “Unleash the beast from the abyss…”

## Type
UI: system-ui / Inter. Code: ui-monospace. Headlines can be a condensed grotesque; do not pair script fonts with the mark.

## Mark
Tentacle-K only. Files:

- `website/img/kit-2026/mark-tentacle-k.jpg` (+ .webp)
- `website/img/kit-2026/icon-512.jpg` / `icon-1024.jpg`
- `website/img/kit-2026/og-1200x630.jpg`
- `website/img/kit-2026/banner-lockup.jpg`

Clear space = one arm-width around the K. Do not put the K on white without an ink plate. Do not add extra eyes, crowns, or exchange-style tentacles.

## Spokesperson
Nyx is optional stills + voice kit (`docs/NYX.md`, `docs/VOICE_KIT.md`). Training videos may use voice over terminal. Do not lip-sync her into every page.
