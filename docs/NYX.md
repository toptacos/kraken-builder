# Nyx Hale

Fictional spokesperson for Kraken by Topta. Relatable to developers. Not a campaign model.

## Who she is

Nyx Hale, 34. Grew up on the North Carolina Piedmont, left for contract ops work, came back because the machines she trusted were always the ones in the room with her. She does not sell cloud. She sells a binary you can hold.

Voice (now): even, informational, a little excited about the command — not perk, not whisper. She shows the command, then why.

Look (now, default): she looks her best. Teal undercut, octopus earring, rested, engaged. Files: `img/nyx-real/now.jpg`, `now-teach.jpg`, `office.jpg`. Homepage avatar is cropped from `now.jpg`.

Look (before only): tired desk/lab/lot. Use only when the video or page is explicitly “without Kraken.” Never on the homepage hero, nav, or courses except the before chapter.

Abyss blazer plates: paid ads only.

## Story (public, short)

She used to keep a folder called `scripts/`. Every client got a new wrapper. Every wrapper broke on someone else’s laptop. Kraken is the folder she wished she had: one runner, arms from any repo, keys that unlock features instead of locking files.

Tagline she is allowed to say:

> Kraken is one binary. You add tentacles. The machine stays yours.

## Story (internal, do not dump on the homepage)

The earring is a copy of a clasp from a dive shop in Morehead City. She does not dive for content. The abyss in the campaign stills is the product metaphor — depth you do not have to live in. Courses are her walking a junior through `doctor` so they do not film themselves.
